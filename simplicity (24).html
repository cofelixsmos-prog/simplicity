Harness

Work sized to the task.

A quick question gets one or two agents and a fast answer. Something like rocket engine configurations, or a long running debate, can summon up to 80 subagents working in parallel, each with a role, searching, analysing, and checking each other's output for hours at a time.

2 agents, light task 80 agents, deep research
01
Assess
Harness reads the task and decides how much work it actually needs.
02
Assign roles
Subagents are given specific jobs including search, analysis, writing, and verification.
03
Work in parallel
Agents share memory and check each other's output as they go.
04
Executor reviews
One executor consolidates everything into a single, checked result.
