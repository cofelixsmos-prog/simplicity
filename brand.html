Usage Guidelines

Do

  • Use the logo on clean, uncluttered backgrounds
  • Maintain clear space around the logo
  • Use approved color combinations shown above

Don't

  • Stretch, rotate, or distort the logo
  • Change the logo colors outside of approved palettes
  • Place the logo on busy or low-contrast backgrounds
  • Use the logo at sizes smaller than 24px
